### Stairs, Round 8 (plan-stairs, stairs_view, tools/stairs, dialogs/stairs; the items above 1-6 were applied by the integration pass)
For `shell/view3d_panel.rs` (not touched by the stairs builder):
1. **Use `editor::stairs_view::scene_meshes(floor: &Floor) -> Vec<Mesh>`** (or `plan_stairs::tagged_meshes(&obj.stair)` per `stairs_view::load(floor)` object) for every stair, ramp and landing. `StairPart` kept its six variants, so the existing exhaustive match still compiles: a stair-side **railing** (newels, balusters, rails) is tagged `Handrail`, and a **wall or half-wall** along a side is tagged `Stringer`. Landing slabs are now `Landing` parts with the landing's real top height.
2. **Remove the `if obj.is_landing()` branch of `stair_meshes`.** It draws a landing at the finished-floor height from its footprint; landings now carry their own height (a landing between two stair sections sits at the height of the section that arrives on it) and are meshed by the engine (`StairShape::Landing`, polygon outlines included).
3. `obj.stair.floor_elevation = floor.elevation;` is harmless: the base of a section that starts on a landing is the new `Stair::base` (added to `floor_elevation` by `Stair::bottom_elevation()`), not a different `floor_elevation`.

## Textures and images (plan-library `image`, plan-materials `textures`, plan-view3d, plan-render)
- `plan_library::image::decode(bytes)` reads PNG (all types and depths, `tRNS`, Adam7) and JPEG (baseline and progressive, 4:4:4/4:2:2/4:2:0, restart markers, CMYK/YCCK; EXIF orientation is ignored) into `Rgba8Image`; `downscaled`, `resized_box` and `mipmaps` filter in linear light. A 2048 x 2048 4:2:0 JPEG decodes in about 90 ms (dev profile, `opt-level = 1`), well under the 150 ms release / 1 s dev budget (`cargo test -p plan-library chief_textures -- --ignored --nocapture` also times real Chief files).
- **Underlay builder:** `tools/underlay/jpeg.rs` is a baseline-only decoder (it refuses progressive files). `plan_library::image::jpeg::decode` covers both, so `underlay/jpeg.rs` can become a thin wrapper (`jpeg::decode(d).map(|i| Rgba { width: i.width as usize, height: i.height as usize, pixels: i.rgba })`). That file is not touched here. `shell/library_browser/png.rs::decode` already delegates to the shared PNG decoder.
- `plan_materials::textures::TextureStore` (use `TextureStore::shared()`): `material(Material)` gives Chief's file when found, else a generated texture; `definition(&MaterialDef)` honours `texture_path`; `file(path, scale)` loads any picture. LRU cache by bytes (512 MB).
- 3D view: `Viewport3d::textures_enabled` (the Standard and Physically Based techniques; `view3d_panel::technique_shows_textures`), `set_image_textures` for pictures (`view3d_panel/textures.rs` decodes PNG and JPEG pictures), a "Textures" check box in the 3D toolbar. Chief textures are decoded on a background thread when a scene first needs them; uploads run at most two per frame. `Viewport3d::context_lost()` re-uploads everything.
- Ray tracer: `RenderSettings::textures` (default on; Physically Based only), `Renderer::with_texture_store` for tests. **Open:** pictures are still flat quads in the ray tracer (`Tri` carries no UV).
- Open: bump/normal maps, per-object material overrides (the Material Painter's colors) do not carry textures yet, and no texture rotation/offset/mirror (Chief's Material Definition has them).

## Roof detail, Roof Defaults and room roof rules (builder pass)
- **Roof Defaults**: `PlanDefaults::roof_detail` (`RoofDetailDefaults`, serde default): thickness, eave cut (plumb / level / square), fascia, soffit, frieze, ridge caps, gutters, flashing, exposed rafter tails (spacing, width, depth), Auto Attic Walls, the attic and lower wall types, Roof Cuts Wall at Bottom and the baseline-at-plate rule (on in Daniel's template). Edit > Default Settings > Roofs > Roof Defaults opens the page; it is drawn from `defaults::show_templates_page` (the per-frame hook the Templates page already uses), so `main.rs` needs nothing. OK can also give the roofs of the open plan the new detail (one undo step, `roof_view::apply_detail`). The Build Roof dialog has the same form on its Detail tab.
- **Where the detail lives**: Build Roof copies it into the roof settings record of its floor (`"detail"`), and `plan_3d::read_floor_roof` reads it back, so the 3D view needs no defaults. Roofs stored before the detail existed keep the eave-tip baseline. A plane's own choices (eave cut, rafter tails, fascia, soffit, frieze, gutters: default / on / off) are the `"eave"` object of its record (`plan_3d::EaveOverrides`), set on the Roof Plane Specification's Options tab.
- **Baseline at the top plate**: `plan_roof::{plate_baseline, build_roof_at_plate}` seat the underside of the structure on the plate at the wall (the reference edge is the first plain hip edge), so gable corners no longer stand about 6" above the plate. The older tests that measure the eave tip use `eave_tip_defaults()`.
- **Walls under and over roofs** (`plan-3d`): half, pony, foundation and curved-facet walls are cut by the roof like standard walls (`FloorCover::wall_top_clipped`, never raised to a gable); Roof Cuts Wall at Bottom cuts a wall's bottom along the top surface of a roof on a lower floor (`bottom_cut`); the Lower Wall Type applies under a butting roof's line and the attic wall type above the plate (`wall::Split`).
- **Rooms**: dropped garage floors and rooms with a Stem Wall get concrete stem walls in 3D (`slab::stem_walls`); Roof Over This Room off leaves the room out of Build Roof's footprint (its partition becomes the roof edge) or cuts a hole in the plane over it; `RoomMisc::flat_roof` ("Flat Roof Over This Room") builds a level plane at the room's ceiling (`plan_roof::flat_roof_plane`); the Flat Roof room function has no ceiling and a membrane deck.
- **Reference Display**: `reference_display::reference_walls` is the one list of reference walls that draw and snap; `snap::snap_with_reference` adds their ends and crossings (called from `EditorContext::snap_at`); `Layer::reference` is the Ref column of the layer table (`shell/docks.rs`). Shift+Cmd+Y is bound to Floor Defaults in `hotkeys::alt_defaults`.
- Still open: Roof Cuts Wall at Bottom follows the centerline of straight walls (a curved wall's bottom stays flat); one material split per wall (a butting roof wins over the plate split); an unsplit exterior wall that spans a roofless room and a roofed one keeps its roof (the editor splits walls at tees); a roofless room that crosses two planes gets no hole; the flat roof plane has no overhang; Auto Rebuild reruns when the room flags change, but a changed Roof Defaults only reaches roofs through the OK checkbox or the next Build Roof.
