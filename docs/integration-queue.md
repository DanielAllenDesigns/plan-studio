### Stairs, Round 8 (plan-stairs, stairs_view, tools/stairs, dialogs/stairs; the items above 1-6 were applied by the integration pass)

## Wall bottom offset (plan-core `Wall.bottom_offset`, plan-3d `wall.rs` / `wall_kinds.rs`, `dialogs/wall.rs`)
