# Chief X18 Reference Manual audit, part 4: Multiple Floor Levels, Stairs Ramps and Landings, Roofs, Framing, Trusses, Trim and Moldings, The Library, Symbol Objects, Architectural Blocks, Other Objects (manual pages 762 to 1098)

Written 2026-10-08 on branch wip/round-14-partial (HEAD 129890a, with the Round 15 builders still editing the tree). Daniel's purpose: find every Chief feature that is not installed yet. This is a docs-only audit: no Rust was edited and no cargo was run.

**Method.** Every page of the Reference Manual from 762 to 1098 was read from the extracted text at `~/plan-studio-dev/chief-docs/` (read only; nothing of it is copied here, every row below is in our own words and only dialog, tool, panel and field names are Chief's). For each feature the status was taken in this order: `docs/chief-feature-coverage.md`, the `docs/parity/*.md` rows (including "Coverage audit additions" and the "Manual audit additions" of parts 1 to 3), then the code (`crates/`, read only). Pages 762 to 773 are the floor chapter, 774 to 821 stairs, 822 to 879 roofs, 880 to 935 framing, 936 to 963 trusses, 964 to 989 trim and moldings, 990 to 1021 the library, 1022 to 1056 symbols, 1057 to 1062 architectural blocks and 1063 to 1098 other objects. (The manual's chapter numbers jump from 25 to 28 in this range; no pages are missing.)

**Status words.** Works; Partial (present with a stated gap); Missing; Differs (by design or by a different mechanism); Out-of-scope (vendor machines, downloads, online stores); In progress (Round 15) when a brief in `~/plan-studio-dev/briefs/r15` covers it and the code is not finished (stairs, ramps and landing tabs, the library dock and its menus). Evidence names a file or function, a test, or a parity id; "NO SPEC -> ID" marks a feature that had no parity row and now has the new id, appended under "Manual audit additions (part 4)" in the matching `docs/parity/*.md` file and in `docs/parity-status.md`.

**Page numbers** are the printed page numbers of the manual (the numbers in its table of contents).

**Cross references.** Roof Group, Framing Group, Shelf Ceiling, the Room Specification Structure fields and the Tray Ceiling dialog belong to part 3 (R-112, R-114, R-115); Convert Polyline, Edit Area, Trim and Extend belong to part 1 (CAD-104, CAD-105, S-177, S-180, S-182); Elevation References are R-89; 3D model export is L-55.

Contents of this file: 22 Multiple Floor Levels; 23 Stairs, Ramps and Landings; 24 Roofs; 25 Framing; 28 Trusses; 29 Trim and Moldings; 30 The Library; 31 Symbol Objects; 32 Architectural Blocks; 33 Other Objects; then "Dialog panels" (panel by panel comparison), "Gaps to build" (ranked), "Counts" and "Where the manual disagrees with what the project assumed".

## Where the manual disagrees with what the project assumed

DECISIONS.md rows and parity statements that the manual contradicts or does not support (verify in Chief where the manual is ambiguous):

- **DECISIONS 116** (Roof Styles in Build Roof are buttons that write every exterior wall's roof directive; also RF-3 and RF-4): the manual (p. 834) says the ROOF STYLES panel only links to online tutorials, one per style. The Hip, Gable, Shed, Gambrel, Dutch Gable and Half Hip buttons are a Plan Studio convenience, not a Chief behaviour; the wall Roof panel remains Chief's real mechanism (p. 407, 826).
- **DECISIONS 112** (Build Framing dialog has tabs Floor, Ceiling, Roof, Wall, Posts, Trusses, Framing Defaults): the manual describes the Build Framing dialog (p. 914 to 915) as three things only: an Automatic Framing Defaults button, Automatically Rebuild Framing choices and Build Framing Once choices with a floor selector. The panel list belongs to the Automatic Framing Defaults dialog (p. 889: Foundation, floor levels, Deck, Deck Support, Wall, Openings, Fireplaces, Roof, Trusses).
- **DECISIONS 122 and RF-36** (a plane's Structure > Define is a sheet of fields): the manual's Roof Surface, Roof Structure and Roof Ceiling Finish definitions (p. 1088 to 1091) are layer tables with Material, Fill, Role and Thickness columns, plus purlin and framing options.
- **DECISIONS 118** (RF-9: roof wings grouped by plate height automatically): the manual (p. 826) groups buildings by a Roof Group number the user sets on rooms, and otherwise roofs the whole exterior as one integrated system. Plate-height grouping is a sensible default but not Chief's rule.
- **DECISIONS 305** (the ceiling platform is one solid block, no separate drywall layer): the manual (p. 768 to 770, 1089) builds ceiling platforms from Ceiling Structure and Ceiling Finish layer definitions, where a dropped ceiling is plenum, framing and drywall layers.
- **DECISIONS 208** (split level: a riser closes the open side; walls that change height along a step are not built): the manual (p. 771) closes the gap with a short generated wall unless Generate Between Platforms is off or the wall has a single layer (W-63 is Partial for the same reason).
- **DECISIONS 6** (Click Stairs tool): the manual's stair tool list (p. 775) has no Click Stairs tool; one click with Draw Stairs places a flight to the next floor and Straight Stairs does the same between rooms and to terrain. Alt reverses the arrow (p. 778) and Ctrl/Cmd stops the wall snaps (p. 777, 780).
- **DECISIONS 53, 70** (Alt suspends snaps), extended from part 1: on the stair tools Alt reverses direction and draws downward (p. 778, 779), and Ctrl/Cmd is the snap override.
- **DECISIONS 58** (Style tab Flared Bottom Tread and Bullnose Bottom Tread; also CB-26 where the Flare/Curve Stairs button toggles winders): in the manual (p. 783, 789 to 794) Flare/Curve Stairs is an edit mode with flare handles on the section's corners and curved treads; Starter Tread is a separate edit mode that rounds the first and second treads; winders are the Winders check box (p. 794).
- **CB-30** (Auto Stairwell makes a room named Stairwell, not Open Below): the manual (p. 800) makes it an Open Below room labelled Stairwell, so the floor and ceiling rules of that function apply.
- **CB-34 and the ramp rule** (a ramp over 30 inches of rise gets landings every 30 inches): the manual (p. 782, 776) limits an automatic ramp to 30 inches of top height and says a taller ramp needs Automatic Heights switched off; it does not say landings are added.
- **R-68** (the attic floor gets an attic room): the manual (p. 773) says rooms cannot be created on the Attic floor and warns when walls or objects are drawn there.
- **RF-44** (Gable/Roof Line tool turns a clicked eave into a gable): the manual (p. 861 to 863) draws a line object parallel to a wall within 10 feet, kept until the next Build Roof.
- **Manual against itself**: p. 853 says roof trusses cannot be generated automatically, while p. 900 and p. 937 describe automatically generated roof trusses (Include Peak Trusses, spacing, hip girder). The code follows the second reading; verify in Chief.
