### Stairs, Round 8 (plan-stairs, stairs_view, tools/stairs, dialogs/stairs; the items above 1-6 were applied by the integration pass)
For `shell/view3d_panel.rs` (not touched by the stairs builder):
1. **Use `editor::stairs_view::scene_meshes(floor: &Floor) -> Vec<Mesh>`** (or `plan_stairs::tagged_meshes(&obj.stair)` per `stairs_view::load(floor)` object) for every stair, ramp and landing. `StairPart` kept its six variants, so the existing exhaustive match still compiles: a stair-side **railing** (newels, balusters, rails) is tagged `Handrail`, and a **wall or half-wall** along a side is tagged `Stringer`. Landing slabs are now `Landing` parts with the landing's real top height.
2. **Remove the `if obj.is_landing()` branch of `stair_meshes`.** It draws a landing at the finished-floor height from its footprint; landings now carry their own height (a landing between two stair sections sits at the height of the section that arrives on it) and are meshed by the engine (`StairShape::Landing`, polygon outlines included).
3. `obj.stair.floor_elevation = floor.elevation;` is harmless: the base of a section that starts on a landing is the new `Stair::base` (added to `floor_elevation` by `Stair::bottom_elevation()`), not a different `floor_elevation`.
4. Stairwell: nothing to wire. The hole is a `PlatformHole { kind: Floor, owner: Some(stair id) }` in the floor above's `foundation` slot, so `plan_3d::add_floor` already cuts it and `project_hash` (which hashes `f.foundation` and `f.stairs`) rebuilds the view. 5. `dialogs::stairs::StairDialog::new(obj)` is unchanged; the Fit to Floor-to-Floor button uses `StairExtras::story_rise` recorded when the stair was drawn, so `spec_dialogs` needs no floor context.

## Textures and images (plan-library `image`, plan-materials `textures`, plan-view3d, plan-render)
